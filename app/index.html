    Roadmap to Platinum

    Earn in this order. The story cannot be missed, the one missable trophy is done early on purpose, and the long grinds come last when you know the map.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story (12 trophies)

      Chapters 1 to 5 plus the big set-piece missions: Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory. Nothing here can be missed, so just enjoy the story — but do Stage 2's balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends.

    2. Stage 2 — The one missable trophy first

      Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy that can be missed: meditate at all 18 spots, including Lucia's balcony meditation on the morning after Down to Earth in Chapter 1. It disappears when Chapter 1 ends, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy is still obtainable without it. Keep a separate Chapter 1 save as insurance.

    3. Stage 3 — Collectibles (4 trophies)

      Card sets, WAiNK location scans, vehicle scans and full map exploration. Scan everything new as you travel and these finish themselves while you play.

    4. Stage 4 — Activities & side strands

      Races, stunts, shooting ranges, robberies, stranger strands (Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and the big counters: 250 stunts, 50 challenges, 500 Criminal Profile actions. The Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is time-limited — do it the moment it appears.

    5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & 100%

      Finish the 100% checklist (Progress → Stats), mop up the last counters, and the Platinum pops when the 54th trophy lands. Roughly 200 hours all in, by guide estimates.
